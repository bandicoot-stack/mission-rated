# Current Spec: Core Platform

## Purpose

Mission Rated is a military-family discovery and decision-support experience for local places, businesses, schools, deals, events, and related high-value journeys.

## Primary audiences

- military members;
- veterans;
- spouses and families;
- military-connected and spouse-owned businesses.

## Core navigation and discovery contract

The homepage prioritizes these discovery paths:

1. Today’s Deals
2. Labor Day / active major seasonal experience
3. Local Deals
4. Everyday Deals
5. Places
6. Businesses
7. Schools

Buy a Car is an integrated Mission Rated journey rather than a separate product identity.

## Trust contract

- Ratings and verification are independent of payment.
- Paid placements are clearly disclosed.
- Research-backed facts retain source/provenance signals.
- AI-assisted enrichment must not create unsupported facts, reviews, rankings, endorsements, or verification.
- Homepage deal cards distinguish the stored offer source from the business website: source-backed primary actions are labeled as verification/use actions, while business-site-only fallbacks do not imply source verification.

## Discovery contract

Public pages intended for discovery should expose appropriate canonical URLs, robots/indexing behavior, semantic metadata, and structured data when applicable.

## Experience contract

- Mobile-first responsive behavior is required.
- Search/filter controls should match the active user context.
- High-value next-step actions should be available without overwhelming the primary task.
- Duplicate directions, repetitive source notices, and unnecessary visual clutter should be avoided.

## Release contract

Production-bound changes are expected to pass Mission Rated QA and Mission Rated Integration QA and receive production verification after deployment. Repository enforcement is tracked separately until branch/ruleset protection is enabled.
