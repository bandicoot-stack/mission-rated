# Proposal: Fall Mission Rated attributes

## Desired outcome

Give Fall Deals & Finds the same core Mission Rated trust and community signals used elsewhere: MR status, source/verification badges, military-value visibility, ownership attributes when verified, and real up/down community voting.

## Acceptance criteria

- Fall venue cards resolve to Mission Rated business records.
- Cards show MR Building or a real MR score only when present in data.
- Active source-backed military offers display on the fall page with a verification link.
- Venues without a confirmed military offer say the discount is not yet confirmed; no offer is invented.
- Up/down arrows use the existing quick-rank vote endpoints and persist the visitor selection.
- Community votes do not create an MR score or User Verified badge.
- Mobile touch targets and focus states remain usable.
