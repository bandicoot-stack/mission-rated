# Proposal: Featured Partner impact targeting

## Problem
Mission Rated Featured Partner outreach can drift toward businesses that are military-adjacent but do not materially help military households save money or make better consumer decisions. Industrial, government-facing, and B2B businesses may be locally relevant without producing strong household impact.

## Change
Require a military-family impact screen before creating a new Featured Partner first-touch draft. Prospects must have a clear household use case, plausible consumer savings or practical value, Hampton Roads military-family relevance, and a consumer-facing path to launch.

Use this prospecting score:
- 40% household savings potential
- 25% military-family audience fit
- 15% frequency of use
- 10% local brand awareness
- 10% speed/ease of launching a useful offer

Default first-wave targets should generally have a credible path to about $10+ in household savings per typical transaction, a strong percentage discount on a commonly used service, or unusually high practical value. This is an internal prioritization heuristic only and must never be represented as an actual merchant offer before confirmation.

## Priority categories
Family attractions and activities, dining, childcare/youth programs, fitness, automotive services, home services, moving/storage, pet care, local retail, seasonal events, and high-ticket household services.

## Deprioritized categories
Industrial suppliers, defense contractors, shipyard support companies, commercial machine shops, government-facing vendors, and similar organization-first businesses unless they have a meaningful consumer-facing offer.

## Trust constraints
Do not fabricate dollar savings, discount percentages, exclusivity, eligibility, or merchant intent. Featured placement remains independent of ratings and rankings.
