# Skill: Partner Outreach

## Use when
Finding, preparing, sending, or following up with potential Mission Rated partners.

## Procedure
1. Research the target with `public-research.md`.
2. Check native Gmail state for prior outreach before drafting.
3. Personalize around Mission Rated's military-family savings mission and the target's verified public relevance.
4. Ask for a concrete military benefit or partnership conversation without inventing terms.
5. If founder requested review-before-send, save drafts only and stop at the approval gate.
6. After sending, record only safe pipeline state in the work queue; do not copy private correspondence into Git.
7. On reply, read native Gmail state, summarize the ask, and draft a response. Preserve any approval gate.

## Output contract
- target
- why fit
- prior-contact check
- proposed subject/body or reply
- send state: `draft_only`, `approved_to_send`, or `sent`
- next action

## Guardrails
Never send when review-before-send is active. Never broaden offer eligibility or claim a partnership until directly confirmed.
