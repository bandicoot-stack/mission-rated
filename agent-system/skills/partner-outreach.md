# Skill: Partner Outreach

## Use when
Finding, preparing, sending, or following up with potential Mission Rated partners.

## Featured Partner target gate
Do not create a new Featured Partner first-touch draft until the prospect passes a military-family impact screen.

Before outreach, be able to clearly state:
- the household use case for a military member, veteran, spouse, or family;
- the likely consumer savings or practical value;
- why the business is relevant to Hampton Roads military households;
- whether the business serves consumers directly rather than primarily government, industrial, or B2B buyers.

Score prospects on:
- 40% household savings potential;
- 25% military-family audience fit;
- 15% frequency of use;
- 10% local brand awareness;
- 10% speed/ease of launching a useful offer.

Prioritize consumer categories such as family attractions and activities, restaurants, childcare and youth programs, fitness, automotive services, home services, moving/storage, pet care, local retail, seasonal events, and high-ticket household services.

Deprioritize industrial suppliers, defense contractors, shipyard support firms, commercial machine shops, government-facing vendors, and other businesses whose primary customer is an organization rather than a household unless they have a meaningful consumer-facing offer.

As a default, a prospect should have a credible path to at least about $10 in household savings on a typical transaction, a strong percentage discount on a commonly used service, or unusually high practical value before it enters the first-wave Featured Partner outreach queue. This is a targeting heuristic, not a claim about an actual offer.

## Procedure
1. Research the target with `public-research.md`.
2. Apply the Featured Partner target gate before creating a new first-touch draft.
3. Check native Gmail state for prior outreach before drafting.
4. Personalize around Mission Rated's military-family savings mission and the target's verified public relevance.
5. Ask for a concrete military benefit or partnership conversation without inventing terms.
6. If founder requested review-before-send, save drafts only and stop at the approval gate.
7. After sending, record only safe pipeline state in the work queue; do not copy private correspondence into Git.
8. On reply, read native Gmail state, summarize the ask, and draft a response. Preserve any approval gate.

## Output contract
- target
- why fit
- military-family use case
- likely impact/value hypothesis
- prior-contact check
- proposed subject/body or reply
- send state: `draft_only`, `approved_to_send`, or `sent`
- next action

## Guardrails
Never send when review-before-send is active. Never broaden offer eligibility or claim a partnership until directly confirmed. Never fabricate a savings amount; the impact screen is a prospecting heuristic and must not be presented as a verified merchant offer.
