# Skill: Featured Partner Onboarding

## Use when
A directly confirmed partner should be added or updated in Mission Rated Featured Partners.

## Procedure
1. Verify direct confirmation and exact offer terms from the native source.
2. Verify public facts and official URLs with first-party sources.
3. Draft partner copy and obtain founder approval when requested.
4. Do not invent or hotlink logos; use the canonical local asset workflow or the documented fallback.
5. Implement through the shared featured-partner data/renderer, not a one-off page.
6. Preserve ratings/sponsorship independence and provenance language.
7. Add/update tests, run QA, open PR, merge after checks, verify production.
8. Update durable queue/checkpoint state.

## Required evidence
- confirmation source safely described
- exact eligibility/offer terms
- official business URL/location facts
- approved copy state
- PR/check/deployment evidence

## Guardrails
Featured placement never changes ratings or organic ranking. Do not broaden military eligibility, redemption method, or partner claims beyond what was confirmed.
