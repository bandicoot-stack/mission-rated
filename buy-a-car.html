Anti-Shark Playbook

1. Get financing before the dealership.
Compare your bank or credit union approval against dealer financing. Negotiate the car price separately.
2. Ask for the out-the-door price.
Price + taxes + required fees + add-ons. Do not let the conversation stay on monthly payment.
3. Compare APR + term + total interest.
A smaller payment can simply mean more months and substantially more interest.
4. Challenge every add-on.
Ask whether each product is optional, what it costs, and whether it changes the financing approval.
5. Leave with the contract numbers.
Verify amount financed, APR, finance charge, total of payments and payment schedule before signing.
