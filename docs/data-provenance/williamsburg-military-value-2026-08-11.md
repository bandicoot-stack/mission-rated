# Williamsburg military-value discovery — 2026-08-11

## Busch Gardens Williamsburg / Water Country USA

**Source class:** first-party business source (Official Source eligible)

**Current active-duty benefit:** Waves of Honor provides one complimentary admission per year for an eligible U.S. active-duty service member and up to three direct dependents. The Williamsburg offer states tickets must be registered online and used by **November 8, 2026**.

**Eligibility:** U.S. Active Duty, Activated or Drilling Reservist, and National Guard currently serving. The first-party page lists Army, Marine Corps, Navy, Air Force, Coast Guard, and Space Force. Valid military ID / ID.me verification requirements apply.

**Additional military value:** eligible active-duty members can obtain up to six discounted Busch Gardens Williamsburg / Water Country USA tickets. Any Day tickets are redeemable within one year of purchase; Multi-Day tickets within six months. Active-duty military discounts are also offered on 1-Park and 2-Park Unlimited Memberships. Base Information, Tickets & Travel offices are an alternate purchase channel for qualifying patrons.

**Restrictions worth displaying:** complimentary admission is not valid for separately ticketed events identified by the park; inactive/standby/retired reserve, military retirees, Merchant Marine, and civilian DoD workers are excluded from the active-duty complimentary Waves of Honor benefit. Do not infer veteran eligibility from the active-duty offer; veteran offers are separate.
