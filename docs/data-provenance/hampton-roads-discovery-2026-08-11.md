## UX/data rules reinforced by this research

1. Preserve **source type** separately from `official_source_verified`: first-party business, government/municipal, DoD, state education, and community evidence are not interchangeable.
2. Offers need explicit `valid_from`, `valid_through`, eligibility, restrictions, and `last_observed_at` where available.
3. Expired seasonal benefits should remain historical provenance but must not rank as current military value.
4. Never convert an authoritative-source benefit into a Mission Rated score by itself; score stays Building/Pending until the rating methodology has sufficient evidence.
5. Rank current user-facing business results by strongest **verified current military value**, while keeping evidence/source links visible.
