## Data handling rule

First-party business URLs verify identity and location only. Public internet ratings must be stored and displayed as external signals with source, observed date, rating scale, and sample size. They must not be relabeled as Mission Rated scores. Mission Rated community scores should publish only after the platform has sufficient first-party community data and a defined confidence threshold.
