# Mission Rated Business QR Distribution Kit

## Goal
Turn participating Hampton Roads businesses into a zero-cost acquisition channel while sending military families directly to useful, source-backed value.

## Core rule
A QR code must deep-link to the most specific verified Mission Rated value available. Never use a QR to imply endorsement, exclusivity, veteran ownership, or a military discount that has not been confirmed.

## Destination hierarchy
1. Verified business-specific military offer.
2. Verified business profile when no offer exists.
3. Relevant seasonal/local guide only when the business is intentionally sharing that community resource.

Avoid sending high-intent scans to the generic homepage.

## Attribution standard
Every business QR destination should carry:

`utm_source=business_qr&utm_medium=offline&utm_campaign=hampton_roads_growth&utm_content=<business_slug>`

If the QR points to a deal, preserve the deal/business identifier separately from UTM parameters so attribution does not depend on display text.

## Counter / window asset copy

**MILITARY DEAL VERIFIED BY MISSION RATED**

Scan to see the current offer, source, and terms.

`[QR CODE]`

Mission Rated helps military families find source-backed local value. Verification is not an endorsement, and businesses cannot pay to change ratings or organic rank.

Use `MILITARY VALUE ON MISSION RATED` instead of `MILITARY DEAL VERIFIED` when the destination is a business profile without a verified offer.

## Mobile landing expectation
A scan should answer these questions without another search:
- What is the business?
- What is the current military offer, if verified?
- When was it verified?
- What source supports it?
- What action should I take next?
- Can I share it with a spouse/friend?
- Can I join the Weekend Brief for future Hampton Roads savings?

## Partner handoff
Provide each participating business only its own unique QR/deep link plus a print-ready asset. Keep the request simple: place it at checkout, front desk, waiting area, or another natural customer decision point. Participation remains free unless a separate clearly labeled paid product is explicitly agreed later.

## Measurement
Track:
- unique QR landing sessions by `utm_content`
- engaged sessions
- verified-offer/source clicks
- share actions
- confirmed Weekend Brief subscriptions
- defensible deal claims/redemption intents
- documented dollars saved when supported by real transaction/claim inputs
- 7-day returning users

Do not count a scan as a claim, redemption, subscriber, or savings event.

## Pilot
Start with 5–10 confirmed Hampton Roads partners with strong military-family fit. Give every partner a unique `utm_content` value. Run long enough to collect directional traffic, then concentrate placement and partner outreach on QR locations producing confirmed downstream actions rather than raw scans.

## Trust guardrails
- No fabricated savings or redemption counts.
- No `exclusive` language without explicit evidence.
- No veteran-owned or spouse-owned language without verification.
- No paid placement may alter Mission Score, verification, reviews, or organic rank.
- Remove or update QR assets when the linked offer expires or loses source support.
