# Mission Rated — 30-Day Hampton Roads Acquisition Sprint

## Objective
Prove a repeatable Hampton Roads military-family acquisition loop before geographic expansion.

## North-star funnel
1. Qualified visitor
2. Family Pass or high-intent content view
3. Weekend Brief signup
4. Share / referral
5. Referred visitor
6. Return visit
7. Community contribution or business conversion

## 30-day targets
| Metric | Target |
|---|---:|
| Qualified visitors | 2,500 |
| Weekend Brief subscribers | 500 |
| Family Pass share actions | 100 |
| Exclusive Family Pass partners | 10 |
| Returning-user rate | 25%+ |
| Active creator sources | 20 |
| Active business distribution sources | 25 |
| High-intent PCS landing visits | 500 |

## Channel hierarchy
1. Business distribution — featured businesses and military-friendly businesses share their Mission Rated destination.
2. Military spouse / family communities — useful guides first; no generic promotional spam.
3. Local creators — curated Mission Rated paths with source attribution.
4. Weekend Brief — weekly retention and referral loop.
5. Search / AI discovery — PCS, schools, neighborhoods, military value, and local-weekend intent.

## Built surfaces
- `/family-pass.html` — free Military Family Pass
- `/pcs-hampton-roads.html` — high-intent PCS acquisition
- `/business-share-kit.html` — tracked business distribution link/copy generator
- `/creator-guides.html` — tracked creator link/copy generator
- Weekend Brief confirmed-signup referral flow
- Sitewide Family Pass acquisition CTA

## Attribution standard
Every distributed Mission Rated link must contain:
- `utm_source`: channel class (`partner`, `creator`, `community`, `email`, etc.)
- `utm_medium`: distribution mechanism
- `utm_campaign`: stable source identifier whenever possible
- `mr_ref`: only for Mission Rated-generated referral tokens

Never append Mission Rated visitor IDs to shared URLs.

## Weekly decision rule
At the end of each week, rank channels by:
1. Confirmed Weekend Brief signups per 100 visits
2. Return visits per 100 acquired visitors
3. Referral visits generated
4. Cost / manual effort per confirmed signup

Double down on the top two channels. Fix or pause channels that produce traffic without confirmed signups or return visits.

## Exclusive Family Pass partner definition
A partner counts toward the 10-partner goal only when the business has explicitly agreed to an offer that is specific to Mission Rated or is otherwise explicitly authorized for promotion through Mission Rated. Do not label ordinary public military discounts as Mission Rated-exclusive.

## Community distribution operating rule
Community posts should lead with a useful artifact (weekend guide, PCS guide, Family Pass, seasonal guide) and disclose Mission Rated ownership/affiliation where appropriate. Follow group rules and moderator requirements; do not automate posting into private groups.

## Exit criteria
Do not expand to another military market until Hampton Roads demonstrates either:
- 500 confirmed Weekend Brief subscribers and 25%+ return rate, or
- a clearly stronger acquisition signal documented from the sprint.
