# Labor Day 2026 — Zero-Cost Distribution Experiment

## Objective
Use the existing `/labor-day` seasonal guide as the primary Hampton Roads acquisition landing page through September 7, 2026. Convert qualified military-family traffic into deeper Mission Rated usage, deal discovery, sharing, and Weekend Brief subscriptions without paid acquisition.

## Why this asset
Labor Day is time-bound, locally relevant, naturally shareable, and already contains source-backed Hampton Roads event and military-value information. It gives Mission Rated a useful reason to be shared that is stronger than a generic homepage pitch.

## Primary audience
- Active-duty households in Hampton Roads
- Military spouses and families planning the long weekend
- Veterans looking for local military value
- Local veteran/military organizations that routinely share useful community resources

## Landing path
External/referral discovery → `/labor-day` → Military Value / This Week → Weekend Brief signup → return visit.

Do not send seasonal traffic to a generic homepage when the Labor Day guide directly matches intent.

## Distribution message
**Hampton Roads military families: Labor Day weekend, sorted.** Mission Rated pulled together source-backed local events, free options, military value, and planning notes for September 4–7. Details link back to the original sources so families can verify before they go.

CTA: `See the Hampton Roads Labor Day guide`

Destination: `https://www.missionratedhq.com/labor-day?utm_source=<channel>&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=labor_day_2026`

## Channel experiments
Run only with founder approval where posting or outreach is required.

### A. Partner share
Ask participating veteran-owned / military-friendly businesses and organizations to share the guide as a useful community resource. Do not require a Mission Rated listing, payment, endorsement, or reciprocal promotion.

Suggested attribution:
- `utm_source=partner`
- `utm_medium=organic`
- `utm_campaign=labor_day_2026`

### B. Military spouse/family community share
Use only groups where resource sharing is permitted. Lead with utility, not promotion. Do not mass-post or repeat the same message across groups in a spam-like pattern.

Suggested attribution:
- `utm_source=mil_family_community`
- `utm_medium=organic`
- `utm_campaign=labor_day_2026`

### C. Founder social
One useful founder post centered on the guide and the Mission Rated trust rule: source-backed details, verify before going, ratings never sold.

Suggested attribution:
- `utm_source=founder_social`
- `utm_medium=organic`
- `utm_campaign=labor_day_2026`

### D. Business QR
For participating businesses, QR should deep-link to the relevant verified offer when one exists. If there is no business-specific offer, a seasonal QR may point to `/labor-day` with a unique partner source tag. Never imply endorsement or exclusivity without evidence.

## Product changes with highest acquisition leverage
1. Add native/copy sharing to `/labor-day` using the existing `deal-share.js` referral helper or a generalized equivalent.
2. Add a visible Weekend Brief signup after the first high-value section and again near the bottom.
3. Add a concise `Send this weekend plan to your spouse/friend` CTA on mobile.
4. Preserve UTM/referral parameters through navigation to Military Value and This Week where practical.
5. Measure confirmed Weekend Brief subscriptions, not form-submit attempts.

## Measurement
Primary funnel:
`landing_session → engaged_session → military_value_or_event_click → share_action → confirmed_weekend_brief_subscription`

Track by `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, and `utm_campaign`.

Report:
- qualified sessions to `/labor-day`
- source/channel
- click-through to Military Value / This Week
- share actions
- confirmed Weekend Brief subscriptions
- deal claims/redemption intents attributable to the campaign
- documented dollars saved attributable to qualifying deal activity
- returning users within 7 days

## Decision rule
After each channel has enough real traffic to be directional, concentrate effort on the source producing the strongest combination of confirmed subscribers, deal engagement, shares, and documented savings. Do not optimize around raw pageviews alone.

## Trust guardrails
- No fabricated urgency, savings, attendance, testimonials, or partner claims.
- Event and deal details remain source-backed and should be rechecked before distribution.
- Do not claim a business is participating unless confirmed.
- Do not treat a browser signup event as a confirmed subscriber until the authoritative subscription succeeds.
- No paid boosting without explicit founder approval.
