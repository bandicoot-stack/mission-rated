# Hampton Roads Organic Distribution Target Map — 2026

## Purpose
Create a zero-paid-acquisition distribution map for Mission Rated's Hampton Roads Growth MVP. These are potential community distribution partners, not endorsements or confirmed Mission Rated partners. No outreach/posting occurs without founder approval.

## Highest-value audience clusters

### 1. Military spouse networks — first priority
These communities have unusually strong fit for the Labor Day guide, Weekend Brief, PCS/local-intelligence content, and deal sharing because members routinely exchange local resources.

- **Surface Officers' Spouses of Norfolk (SOS Norfolk)** — Hampton Roads Navy spouse organization focused on friendship, mentorship, support, and navigating Navy life. Source: https://sosnorfolk.org/
- **Hampton Roads Chief Petty Officer Spouse Association (HRCPOSA)** — supports Navy and Coast Guard Chief spouses and families; explicitly highlights resources, community engagement, networking, and partnerships. Source: https://sites.google.com/view/hrcposa
- **Hiring Our Heroes Hampton Roads/Norfolk Military Spouse Professional Network** — identified by the City of Norfolk as a regional military-spouse career/networking community. Source: https://norfolk.gov/6337/Military-Spouse-Employment-and-Training-

**Best asset:** useful seasonal/local guide first; Weekend Brief second. Do not lead with "join Mission Rated." Lead with a resource worth sharing.

### 2. Military-family support organizations — high reach / high trust
- **Armed Services YMCA of Hampton Roads** — serves military members and families, with particular focus on junior enlisted households. Source directory confirmation: https://search.211virginia.org/search/d0bd97dd-7347-5047-9b83-33d711452424
- **USO of Hampton Roads & Central Virginia** — regional support footprint serving military members and families across Chesapeake, Hampton, Newport News, Norfolk, Portsmouth, Suffolk, and Virginia Beach. Source directory confirmation: https://search.211virginia.org/search/64570c1d-3ccd-530d-8e52-077a1fce815a

**Best asset:** free Hampton Roads weekend/deal resource with source links. Partnership framing should be community utility, not reciprocal promotion.

### 3. Civic military-affairs channels — credibility + broad regional distribution
- **City of Norfolk Office of Veteran Services and Military Affairs** — city office serving active duty, veterans, families and military/federal partnerships. Source: https://www.norfolk.gov/2667/Office-of-Veteran-Services-and-Military-
- **Hampton Military Affairs Committee** — explicitly supports information sharing, resources, opportunities, military families and veterans around JBLE and greater Hampton Roads. Source: https://www.hampton.gov/2476/Military-Affairs-Committee

**Best asset:** source-backed public-service guide. Avoid commercial language and avoid asking for endorsement.

### 4. Veteran networks — secondary distribution
- **Hampton Roads Council of Veterans Organizations** — umbrella-style local veterans organization focused on regional veteran organizations and community programs. Source: https://hrcvo.org/
- **Hampton Roads Veterans Association** — local veteran organization focused on partnerships and community/youth work. Source: https://www.hamptonroadsveterans.org/

**Best asset:** veteran/military savings and community-resource pages, especially when there is concrete verified value.

## Recommended sequencing
1. Founder social post establishes the asset publicly and provides a clean shareable example.
2. Ask existing friendly/known partners to share it voluntarily.
3. Approach 1–2 spouse organizations with a utility-first note and no commercial ask.
4. If the guide generates real engagement, approach military-family nonprofits/civic offices with observed value rather than projected value.
5. Expand only after attribution is trustworthy.

## Measurement gate
Do not rank channels using raw pageviews. Compare only when analytics can defensibly capture source/referral and confirmed downstream outcomes. Primary outcomes:
- qualified landing sessions
- engaged deal/event clicks
- share actions
- confirmed Weekend Brief subscriptions
- attributable deal claims/redemption intent
- documented savings
- 7-day return visits

Until the known growth-analytics allowlist and confirmed-subscription instrumentation issues are fixed, treat share/referral and subscriber conversion reporting as incomplete.

## Trust rules
- These organizations are prospects/distribution targets, not Mission Rated partners unless they explicitly agree.
- Do not imply endorsement.
- Do not mass-post to private/community groups.
- Respect each group's posting rules and moderator decisions.
- Do not offer payment, reciprocal ranking, ratings influence, or review manipulation.
- Every event/deal claim in the distributed asset remains source-backed and current.
