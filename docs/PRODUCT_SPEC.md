# Product specification

## Purpose

Mission Rated is the installation-first local discovery platform for military families. The v1 loop is **Installation → Businesses → Military Deals → Reviews**. Hampton Roads is the launch market.

## Audiences

- Military members and families: free discovery, comparison, deals, and community insight.
- Military spouse-owned businesses: free baseline visibility.
- Commercial businesses: paid, clearly labeled visibility, leads, recruiting, sponsorships, and promotions.

## Non-negotiable trust rules

1. Mission Scores are derived only from approved community signals. Payment, sponsorship, and placement never affect a score.
2. Sponsored content must be labeled at every presentation point.
3. Production reviews, counts, verification, and scores must come from production data; preview content must be explicit.
4. Military-family use remains free.
5. Military spouse-owned business visibility remains free.

## Beta experience

The homepage establishes installation context, searches businesses/services/deals, browses categories, presents business cards, explains score independence, and previews future platform areas. Business cards support name, category, distance, Mission Score, review count, verified deal, verification, and sponsorship status.

## Success signals

- A visitor understands the product and selects an installation within 30 seconds.
- Search and category entry points lead to relevant local businesses.
- Users can distinguish verification, sponsorship, deals, and Mission Score.
- No empty backend state is misrepresented as real community activity.
